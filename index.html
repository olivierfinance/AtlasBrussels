Choose dataset to be displayed

The first datasets describe places according to demographical, economical and morphological features. They may be helpful to contextualize analyses on other datasets revealing diverse kind of interaction basins in Brussels and Belgium.

Contextualization
Demographical, economical and morphological features

Population density
Distribution of population at the place of residence

Jobs density
Distribution of population at the place of work

Built-up density
Distribution of built-up coverage

Land use
Classification of the main land use by basic spatial unit

Interactions
Results of analyses based on interrelations between places (partitions based on community detection algorithms)

Commuting data
Communities based on the intensity of regular journeys made from home- to work-places

Migration data
Communities based on the intensity of permanent house moving between places

Mobile phone calls data
Communities based on the intensity of phone calls between antennas

Trucks circulations
Communities based on the internsity of circulations of trucks (seen as a proxy of goods exchanges)

iRail data
Communities based on the intensity of train trips requests between stations

Twitter data
Communities based on the intensity of reply-to geo-located tweets

Other analyses
Complementary analyses based on similar datasets as previous section

Trucks circulations
Circulations of trucks: trafic and connections approaches

Twitter data
Distribution of reply-to geo-located tweets

How to cite, share and reuse atlas.brussels

To cite and share atlas.brussels

Please use the following reference:

Finance Olivier (2018). atlas.brussels website (Version 1). Available online from https://atlas.brussels

To reuse atlas.brussels

"atlas.brussels" is under two different licences: one for the code, one for the images to upload.

To reuse downloaded images

Each map can be downloaded with the "save as" icon on the bottom right of the page and on the full project website, section "downloadable maps". These maps are protected by a Creative Commons CC-BY-NC-SA 2.0 BE license. This means you can share and adapt the material under the following terms: attribution (you must give appropriate credit to atlas.brussels), NonCommercial (you may not use the material for commercial purposes) and ShareAlike (if you remix, transform, or build upon the material, you must distribute your contributions under the same license as the original). More information about this licence here.

To reuse code

Code created to generate atlas.brussels visualization tool is available under a GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL 3.0). More information about this licence here.
